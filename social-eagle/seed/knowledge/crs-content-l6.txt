# Content Strategy Foundations — Lesson 6: Measuring content performance

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Measure content with three numbers: saves, comments from your target audience, and profile visits. Likes alone are a weak signal. Review the numbers every Friday and keep a 'top five posts' list so you know what to repurpose next month.
