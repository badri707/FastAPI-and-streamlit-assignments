# Short-Form Video Playbook — Lesson 4: Editing workflow

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

A simple editing workflow: cut silences, add captions, add one pattern interrupt every 5 to 7 seconds such as a zoom or b-roll, and export in 9:16 vertical format at 1080x1920. Captions are required because many viewers watch without sound.
