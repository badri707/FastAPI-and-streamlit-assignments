# Content Strategy Foundations — Lesson 5: Building a content calendar

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Build your content calendar one month ahead in a simple spreadsheet with columns for date, pillar, format, hook and status. Batch-create content on one fixed day each week. Leave two empty slots per week for timely or reactive posts.
