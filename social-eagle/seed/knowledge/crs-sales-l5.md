# Sales Calls & Offers — Lesson 5: Follow-up system

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Follow up three times after a call: the same day with a recap, on day 3 with a relevant case study, and on day 7 with a direct question. After the third follow-up, move the lead to your monthly newsletter.
