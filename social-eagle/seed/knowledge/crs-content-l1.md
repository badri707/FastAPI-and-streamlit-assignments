# Content Strategy Foundations — Lesson 1: Choosing your niche

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Pick a niche by combining a specific audience with a specific outcome. Test your niche statement with the 'one sentence' rule: if you cannot say who you help and what result they get in one sentence, the niche is too broad. Validate demand by finding at least five people in that audience already paying for a solution.
