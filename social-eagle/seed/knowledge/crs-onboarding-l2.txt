# Community Onboarding Bootcamp — Lesson 2: Setting up your profile

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Use your real first name and a clear face photo on your profile. Add a one-line bio that names who you help, for example 'I help yoga studios fill weekday classes'. Link one primary social account only. Profiles with a photo and bio receive noticeably more replies in the community, so complete this before posting your first question.
