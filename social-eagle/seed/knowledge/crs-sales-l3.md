# Sales Calls & Offers — Lesson 3: The discovery call script

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

The discovery call script has five stages: rapport (2 minutes), current situation, desired outcome, obstacles, and the invitation. Spend at least 70 percent of the call listening. End by asking 'Would you like help getting there?' before presenting the price.
