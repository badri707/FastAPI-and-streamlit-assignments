# Email Marketing Essentials — Lesson 2: Lead magnets that work

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

A good lead magnet solves one narrow problem quickly, for example a one-page checklist or a 10-minute video. Avoid long ebooks as a first lead magnet. Name it with the result it delivers, such as 'The 7-Day Reel Plan'.
