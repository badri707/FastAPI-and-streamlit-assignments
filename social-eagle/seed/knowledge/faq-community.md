# Community FAQ — access, accounts and support

> SYNTHETIC evaluation FAQ — not actual Social Eagle policy.

## Why does a course show as locked?
Courses are unlocked by membership tier. In this evaluation community, Sales Calls & Offers is available to the annual tier, and AI Tools for Marketers — Advanced Lab is available to the annual and VIP tiers. If a course you expect to see is locked, post in the Help Desk category with your tier and the course name, and a staff member will check your access.

## Videos are not playing
First refresh the page and try another browser. If a lesson video still shows an error, post in Help Desk with the course name, lesson number and a screenshot of the error. Staff review Help Desk posts every weekday.

## Refunds, billing and cancellations
Refund, billing and cancellation requests are handled only by staff through the billing desk. The community assistant cannot process, approve or promise refunds or billing changes.

## Who answers questions?
Members, moderators and an automated assistant. Automated answers are labelled as automated and cite the lesson or FAQ they are based on. Staff can correct or remove automated answers.

## When are office hours?
Office hours run every Tuesday at 7:00 PM IST for 60 minutes. Recordings are posted within 48 hours.
