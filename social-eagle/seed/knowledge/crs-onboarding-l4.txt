# Community Onboarding Bootcamp — Lesson 4: Weekly rhythm: calls and challenges

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

The weekly rhythm has two live touchpoints. Office hours run every Tuesday at 7:00 PM IST for 60 minutes; bring one question. The Friday Build Challenge opens every Friday at 10:00 AM IST and you post your result by Sunday night. Recordings of office hours are added to the calendar post within 48 hours.
