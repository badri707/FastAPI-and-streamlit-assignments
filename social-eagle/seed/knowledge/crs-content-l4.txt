# Content Strategy Foundations — Lesson 4: Repurposing one idea five ways

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Repurpose one core idea five ways: a long post, a carousel, a short video, an email, and a question post to spark comments. Start from your best-performing post of the month. Repurposing should happen over about two weeks so the same audience does not see all five versions on the same day.
