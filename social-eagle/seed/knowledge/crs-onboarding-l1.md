# Community Onboarding Bootcamp — Lesson 1: Welcome and community rules

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Welcome to the community. Three rules: be generous with feedback, no direct-message selling, and keep wins and questions in their matching categories. Posts that pitch services to other members are removed by moderators. Introduce yourself in the Introductions category within your first week using the template: who you help, what you sell, and one goal for the next 90 days.
