# Short-Form Video Playbook — Lesson 2: The first three seconds

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

The first three seconds decide whether people keep watching. Open with movement, on-screen text that names the problem, and start speaking immediately without greetings. Avoid opening with 'Hi guys, welcome back'.
