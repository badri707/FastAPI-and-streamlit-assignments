# Analytics & Reporting — Lesson 2: Setting up UTM links

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Use UTM parameters on every link you share: utm_source for the platform, utm_medium for the format and utm_campaign for the offer. Keep naming in lowercase with hyphens to avoid duplicated rows in reports.
