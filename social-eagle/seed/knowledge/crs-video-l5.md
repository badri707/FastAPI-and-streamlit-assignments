# Short-Form Video Playbook — Lesson 5: Posting cadence for video

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

For short-form video, post at least four videos per week for the first eight weeks to learn what works. Consistency matters more than the exact posting time. After eight weeks, keep the formats that earned the most saves and shares.
