# Sales Calls & Offers — Lesson 4: Handling objections

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Handle objections with the acknowledge-ask-answer pattern. For the price objection, ask what result would make the investment worthwhile before defending the price. Common objections are price, timing, trust and spouse or partner approval.
