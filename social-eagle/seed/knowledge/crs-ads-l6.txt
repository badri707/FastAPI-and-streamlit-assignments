# Paid Ads Fundamentals — Lesson 6: Scaling winners

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Scale winning ad sets by increasing the budget 20 percent every three days. Large sudden budget jumps reset learning and usually raise costs. Duplicate a winner into a new audience instead of editing a running ad set.
