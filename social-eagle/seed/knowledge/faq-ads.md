# Paid Ads FAQ

> SYNTHETIC evaluation FAQ — intentionally contains a statement that conflicts with Lesson 3 for testing.

## What is the minimum daily ad budget?
The minimum daily budget per ad set is 300 INR. Lower budgets make results noisy.

## How long should I wait before judging an ad?
Wait at least 4 days and do not judge results in the first 24 hours.

## Should I boost posts?
Boosting is fine for awareness but use the ads manager for lead campaigns so you can control audiences and tracking.
