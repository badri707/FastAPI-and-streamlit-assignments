# Content Strategy Foundations — Lesson 2: Content pillars

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Content pillars are three to five recurring themes you always post about. A simple starter set is: education (how-to), proof (results and case studies), personal story, and offer. Aim for roughly 40 percent education, 30 percent proof, 20 percent story and 10 percent direct offer posts.
