# Email Marketing Essentials — Lesson 1: Why email still matters

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Email matters because you own the list; social platforms can change reach overnight. An engaged email list of 500 people can generate more sales than 10,000 passive followers. Start collecting emails from day one, even before you have a product.
