# Analytics & Reporting — Lesson 5: Monthly review ritual

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

The monthly review ritual: on the first working day of the month compare last month with the month before, list three wins and three lessons, and choose one experiment for the coming month. Archive the report in a shared folder.
