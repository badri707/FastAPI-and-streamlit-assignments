# Analytics & Reporting — Lesson 1: Metrics that matter

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Track metrics that connect to revenue: leads per week, call bookings, close rate and revenue per client. Vanity metrics such as follower count are secondary. Pick no more than five metrics to review weekly.
