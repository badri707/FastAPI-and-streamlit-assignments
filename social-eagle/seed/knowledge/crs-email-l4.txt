# Email Marketing Essentials — Lesson 4: Subject lines and open rates

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Good subject lines are under 50 characters, specific and curiosity-driven. Avoid all caps and excessive punctuation. A healthy open rate for a small, engaged list is roughly 35 to 50 percent; below 20 percent usually signals deliverability or relevance problems.
