# Email Marketing Essentials — Lesson 5: List hygiene and deliverability

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Clean your list every 90 days by removing subscribers who have not opened any email in that period, after sending them one re-engagement email. Always use double opt-in for new subscribers and authenticate your sending domain with SPF and DKIM.
