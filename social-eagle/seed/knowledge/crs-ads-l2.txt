# Paid Ads Fundamentals — Lesson 2: Audiences and targeting

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Start with one broad audience and one interest-based audience. Exclude existing customers and recent purchasers. Do not stack many interests in one ad set; give the platform room to optimise.
