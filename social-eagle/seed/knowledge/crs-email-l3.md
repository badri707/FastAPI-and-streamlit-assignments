# Email Marketing Essentials — Lesson 3: The five-email welcome sequence

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

The five-email welcome sequence: email 1 delivers the lead magnet immediately; email 2 tells your story; email 3 shares a quick win; email 4 handles a common objection; email 5 makes a soft offer. Send one email per day for the first five days.
