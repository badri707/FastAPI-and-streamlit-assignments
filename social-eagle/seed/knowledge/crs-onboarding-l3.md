# Community Onboarding Bootcamp — Lesson 3: How to ask a good question

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

A good question includes four things: what you are trying to do, what you already tried, what happened, and a screenshot or link where useful. Post questions in the Questions category, not in Wins. Use one question per post so others can answer clearly. If your question is about a specific lesson, name the course and lesson number in the title.
