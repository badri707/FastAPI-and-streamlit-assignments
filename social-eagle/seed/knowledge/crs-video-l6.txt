# Short-Form Video Playbook — Lesson 6: Turning views into leads

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Turn views into leads by pinning a comment with your lead magnet keyword and adding the same keyword to the caption. Reply to every comment within the first hour after posting. Move interested viewers to your email list rather than only to direct messages.
