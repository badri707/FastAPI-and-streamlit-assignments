# Friday Build Challenge FAQ

> SYNTHETIC evaluation FAQ — intentionally conflicts with Onboarding Lesson 4 on the deadline, for testing.

## When does the challenge open?
The Friday Build Challenge opens every Friday at 10:00 AM IST.

## What is the submission deadline?
Submit your challenge result by Saturday at midnight IST.

## Are there prizes?
There are no cash prizes. Featured submissions are highlighted in the weekly recap post.
