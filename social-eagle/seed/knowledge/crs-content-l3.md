# Content Strategy Foundations — Lesson 3: The hook-story-offer framework

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

The hook-story-offer framework has three parts. The hook is the first line and must name a pain or a surprising claim. The story shows a real example or client situation in under 120 words. The offer is a single clear next step, such as 'comment GUIDE to get the checklist'. Never put two different calls to action in one post.
