# Short-Form Video Playbook — Lesson 3: Scripting short videos

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Script short videos in three beats: hook (3 seconds), value (20 to 40 seconds), and call to action (5 seconds). Write the script in spoken language and read it aloud once before recording. Keep each video to a single idea.
