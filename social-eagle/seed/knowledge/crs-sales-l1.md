# Sales Calls & Offers — Lesson 1: Designing a starter offer

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

A starter offer is a small, fast, low-risk service that solves one urgent problem, for example a 2-week content sprint. It should be deliverable in under 14 days and priced so that a client can say yes without a long approval process.
