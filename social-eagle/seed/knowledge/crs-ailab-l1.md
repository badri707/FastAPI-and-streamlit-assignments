# AI Tools for Marketers — Advanced Lab — Lesson 1: Prompt patterns for copy

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Prompt patterns for copy: give the AI a role, the audience, the goal, one example of your voice, and a word limit. Always ask for three variations and edit the best one yourself. Never publish AI copy without a human read-through.
