# Short-Form Video Playbook — Lesson 1: Gear on a budget

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

You can start short-form video with a phone, a clip-on lavalier microphone and a window for natural light. Audio quality matters more than video quality: viewers forgive average video but leave quickly when audio is poor. A ring light is optional, not required, for beginners.
