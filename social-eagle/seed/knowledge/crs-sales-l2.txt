# Sales Calls & Offers — Lesson 2: Pricing your offer

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Pricing your starter offer: for the first 20 clients, price the starter offer between 4,000 and 8,000 INR. Raise the price by 20 percent after every five testimonials. Never discount more than 15 percent; add bonuses instead of cutting price.
