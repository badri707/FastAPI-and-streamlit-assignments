# Paid Ads Fundamentals — Lesson 5: Reading ad metrics

> SYNTHETIC evaluation course material — not actual Social Eagle content.

## Lesson summary

Read ad metrics in this order: cost per result, click-through rate, then cost per thousand impressions. A click-through rate below 0.8 percent usually means the creative or hook needs work. Do not judge results from the first 24 hours.
